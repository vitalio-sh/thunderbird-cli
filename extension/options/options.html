Account access for AI agents

Agents that use thunderbird-cli (CLI or MCP) only see the accounts you allow here. Other accounts behave as if they did not exist: their folders, messages and sender identities are hidden. Agents cannot change this setting.

Address books (contacts) are not tied to accounts and stay available. Accounts added later stay hidden until you check them.

Sending

Off (recommended): agents can only save drafts or open a compose window for you to review and send. A message an agent was tricked into writing (for example by a malicious email) then never leaves without you.
